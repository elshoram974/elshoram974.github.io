MRE CashBook

A cashbook app, daily expense tracker and small business ledger. Record cash in and cash out, track debts per contact, manage product stock, export PDF reports, and keep encrypted backups in your own Google Drive or Huawei Drive. Works fully offline.

Google Drive access & Limited Use

MRE CashBook offers optional cloud backup. It requests only the restricted https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata scope, which can see nothing but the hidden app-data folder the app creates inside your own Drive. It cannot list, read or touch any other file in your Google account, and MRE Code operates no servers that receive your ledger.

Limited Use disclosure. MRE CashBook’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The full detail is in the Google API data disclosure.
